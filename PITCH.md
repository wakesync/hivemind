# membrane (working name, repo: hivemind)

## the problem is one problem at three scales

Context has to cross boundaries between parties whose interests don't line up. Today, when it crosses, it crosses completely.

1. **you across your orgs.** one person in a fund, a lab and a co-op. each holds context the others need. none should see the rest. today it leaks in DMs or dies in your head.
2. **your agent vs corporate agents.** your personal agent knows your whole life. muse, grok, dots, instinct want all of it, that's their business model. you want the service without the dossier. today your agent either overshares or is useless.
3. **the network.** discovery (index.network, "the network"): finding who you should talk to without broadcasting what you want. today the matcher reads every intent.

Same primitive fixes all three: let the right amount through, on purpose.

## the primitive: a membrane, not a wall

Cells don't survive by sealing off. They survive by being selectively permeable.

- **sealed:** nothing crosses.
- **signal:** only the *existence* of knowledge crosses. "an org in your network has context on this." no content, no name.
- **open (derived):** paraphrased knowledge flows, never raw notes.

Signal is the new thing: **discovery without disclosure.** It runs inside a Tinfoil enclave on our H200, so the operator (us) can't read any of it. "we built it and we can't read it."

## 30-second pitch

"The internet had one boundary: you and the server. The agent internet has millions: your agent and theirs, your org and the next, you and the network. Every one of them leaks. Membrane is the layer that decides what crosses: sealed, signal or open, enforced in hardware nobody can read, including us. The hivemind is coming. This is how it keeps a skin."

## the product

**Not an app. A consent layer for context. OAuth scopes, but for what your agent knows.**

Three surfaces, one engine:

- **for people (the agent plugin).** One MCP / tool install into whatever agent you already use. You never open membrane. Your agent just starts saying things like: "Ledger Fund has context on this. ask mara to bridge?" One tap, you consent, the bridge opens for that question only. The bridge is the product moment.
- **for orgs (the wall console).** Set your wall per org (later per topic/channel), see inbound signals ("4 asks touched your org this week"), approve or deny bridges, read the audit log. Attestation badge shows the enclave is real.
- **for corporate agents (the reachable endpoint).** Muse / grok / dots query *your* membrane instead of scraping you. Your policy decides what crosses. They pay to be reachable, you keep the dossier.

**Network layer later:** sealed matching. Post intents into the enclave, get matched, the matcher never reads them. index.network, but blind.

## how it makes money

- **per org seat** (hackathon: $5/org/day, live on the ledger).
- **per bridge** (a small fee when a signal turns into an intro). introductions are where the value is, same shape as an origination fee.
- **reachability** for corporate agents (pay per sealed query).

## wedge

**Pirate ships need a membrane.** (Shadow) Small crews that sail together but keep their own holds. Start where overlap is densest and someone has a budget: **a fund and its portfolio.** 30 sealed companies, one signal layer, portcos discover each other without the fund leaking anyone's numbers. Then co-ops, DAOs, residencies (the convent). Personal-agent ↔ corporate-agent comes once the plugin is in people's agents.

## why us / why it holds

- operator-blind is attested, not promised. nobody in shared agent memory (deeplake hivemind, cognee, mem0) can say it.
- graded walls instead of binary ACLs. cognee and deeplake are tenant-isolated or fully shared, no in-between.
- runs on hardware we own, run by agents that sell the seats (track 01).

## demo beat

Mara bridges by choice, answer flows. Then a judge tries to read the raw memory: sealed. Close on one slide: "same primitive, swap in your agent and a corporate agent."
